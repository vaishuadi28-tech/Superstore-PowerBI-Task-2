DAX MEASURES USED IN THE PROJECT

1. Total Sales
   Formula:
   Total Sales = SUM('Orders'[Sales])
   Why Used:
   - To calculate total revenue across all transactions.
   - Required for KPI cards, bar charts, category-wise comparisons.

2. Total Profit
   Formula:
   Total Profit = SUM('Orders'[Profit])
   Why Used:
   - Shows business profitability.
   - Allows comparison between categories and subcategories.

3. Profit Margin
   Formula:
   Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
   Why Used:
   - Shows how efficiently revenue turns into profit.
   - Helps identify high-profit vs low-profit regions and products.

4. Total Orders
   Formula:
   Total Orders = COUNTROWS('Orders')
   Why Used:
   - Required for KPI card.
   - Helps understand customer activity and order flow.
